Contact us by email for help with LOGOS Flag Quiz, to report an incorrect question or an app issue, or to share feedback.

For an app issue, tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and your device model, operating system version, and app version if available. For an incorrect question, include the question or country name and the information you believe needs correcting.

Include only information relevant to your request. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords or payment details, and send a backup file only if it is needed to investigate the issue.

Privacy

You can use the same email address for privacy questions and requests to review, correct, or delete information you have sent to support. We cannot access or delete quiz data stored only on your device or in backups you control.
