This Privacy Policy explains how Flag Quiz handles data. Flag Quiz is designed as a local-first educational quiz app. The app does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your quiz history in the current version.

1. Data Stored On Your Device

Flag Quiz stores app data on your device so the app can work properly and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • quiz settings
  • language preference
  • custom Lists
  • Mastery records
  • remaining quiz-question access and rewarded-ad credits
  • cached Premium subscription status
  • backup files you choose to export or import

This data is used to provide app features, personalize the quiz experience, and restore your local app state. In the current version, this data is not sent to a developer-owned server.

Your device operating system may include app data in device backups, such as iCloud Backup on iOS or Android backup services, depending on your device settings.

2. Export And Import

Flag Quiz includes export and import features so you can move or back up your app data manually. Exported files may include quiz settings, Lists, and Mastery records.

You control whether to create, share, store, or import these files. If you send an exported backup file to another person or service, that recipient may be able to access the data contained in the file.

3. Rewarded Ads

Flag Quiz may use Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, you may receive additional quiz-question access.

When ads are enabled, Google and its partners may collect or process data such as:

  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • app interactions and ad interactions
  • diagnostics, crash, and performance information
  • advertising-related data used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance

The exact data handled by Google Mobile Ads may depend on your platform, consent choices, device settings, region, and the final ad configuration used in the released app.

For more information about Google's handling of advertising data, see Google's privacy resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob

Where required, the app will request advertising consent before showing ads that require consent.

4. Premium Subscription And Purchases

Flag Quiz Premium may be offered as an auto-renewing subscription. Premium unlocks unlimited quiz access, removes rewarded-ad prompts, and includes Premium features such as Lists and Mastery.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the App Store or Google Play. The developer does not receive your full payment card details. The app may receive and store purchase or entitlement status so it can unlock Premium features.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account settings.

5. No Account System

Flag Quiz does not create a developer-hosted account. The app does not collect passwords, contact lists, or profile information for a developer account system.

6. Children

Flag Quiz is an educational quiz app, but it is not intended to be submitted as a child-directed app or as part of a child-specific store program unless this policy and the app behavior are updated. The app should not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-owned account or server.

7. Data Retention And Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed. You can remove local app data by deleting the app, using available reset/delete features in the app, or replacing data through import where supported.

Purchase records are managed by Apple or Google according to their own policies. Advertising-related data is handled by Google and its partners according to their policies and your consent choices where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services

Flag Quiz may use these third-party services:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit for iOS subscriptions and purchases
  • Google Play Billing for Android subscriptions and purchases
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for rewarded ads

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and platform rules.

9. Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, advertising behavior, purchase behavior, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be made available through the app or the published policy URL.
