This Privacy Policy explains how Flag Quiz | LOGOS handles data. Flag Quiz | LOGOS is designed as a local-first educational quiz app. The app does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your quiz history in the current version.

1. Data Stored On Your Device

Flag Quiz | LOGOS stores app data on your device so the app can work properly and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • quiz settings
  • language preference
  • custom Lists
  • Mastery records
  • backup files you choose to export or import

This data is used to provide app features, personalize the quiz experience, and restore your local app state. In the current version, this data is not sent to a developer-owned server.

Your device operating system may include app data in device backups, such as iCloud Backup on iOS or Android backup services, depending on your device settings.

2. Export And Import

Flag Quiz | LOGOS includes export and import features so you can move or back up your app data manually. Exported files may include quiz settings, Lists, and Mastery records.

You control whether to create, share, store, or import these files. If you send an exported backup file to another person or service, that recipient may be able to access the data contained in the file.

3. Free App Features

The current version of Flag Quiz | LOGOS is free. It does not show advertising, offer in-app purchases or subscriptions, or limit the number of quiz questions you can answer. Lists and Mastery are included without a paid upgrade.

4. No Account System

Flag Quiz | LOGOS does not create a developer-hosted account. The app does not collect passwords, contact lists, or profile information for a developer account system.

5. Children

Flag Quiz | LOGOS is an educational quiz app, but it is not intended to be submitted as a child-directed app or as part of a child-specific store program unless this policy and the app behavior are updated. The app should not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-owned account or server.

6. Data Retention And Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed. You can remove local app data by deleting the app, using available reset/delete features in the app, or replacing data through import where supported.

7. External Services

App distribution and device backups are handled by your platform provider according to its policies and your settings. If you open an external link, email support, or share a backup through another service, that service handles the information you provide according to its own policies.

8. Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data handling, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be made available through the app or the published policy URL.

9. Contact

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you include so that it can respond to your request.
